Command reference

Every command in the simulator, from DAFPAM 34-1203 and the command inflection chart. While marching, say the preparatory command as the heel of the foot shown strikes the ground and the command of execution when that same heel strikes again (DAFPAM 3.9.2). Type it in one line, or in two parts (preparatory, Enter, then execution, Enter) to have both parts graded.

Commands

Command voice

Use the inflection chart: HARCH and HACE. Typed MARCH and FACE are not accepted. Capital letters and commas are optional. Give each command as the correct heel strikes (watch the metronome).

Half step & route step

After a column movement or Counter, HARCH the flight half steps until Forward, HARCH; no flanks or To the Rear while half stepping. At route step / at ease only inclines and Flight, Ten-HUT.

Disorder & COVER

Wrong-foot, off-beat or sloppy commands make cadets drift. Halt, then COVER. After every halt the next command must be COVER.
